### Description — 1302/4000
```
Kökçe tells you where Turkish words come from.

One word is chosen each day. Its origin, the languages it passed through, its first written attestation and its meaning today all sit on one screen.

JOURNEY
Follow a word step by step: where it was born and which languages carried it into Turkish. Each step shows the form and meaning in that language. Reconstructed forms are marked with an asterisk.

DICTIONARY
538 words. Filter by origin language, keep your favourites. Search follows Turkish rules, so "sarki" still finds "şarkı".

SOURCES
Every entry names the source it rests on: Nişanyan Sözlük, the Turkish Language Association's Güncel Türkçe Sözlük, Kubbealtı Lugatı, Tietze. Unknown fields are left empty rather than invented. Where an origin is disputed, the competing view is given too.

HOME SCREEN
See the word of the day in a widget. You never have to open the app.

DAILY REMINDER
The word of the day arrives at the time you choose, in your device's local time.

WORD POOL
Draw the daily word from the mixed, everyday or lesser-known pool. Open the lesser-known pool to stretch your vocabulary.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
No ads, no subscription, no account. The app collects nothing and sends nothing to a server. The code is MIT and the content is CC BY-SA 4.0; both are public on GitHub.
```
